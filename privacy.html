Contact form

The website contact form currently prepares an email in your device’s email application. When you choose to send it, the message is sent through your email provider to app.team.liia@gmail.com. During project work, third-party tools may be used where appropriate. Their own privacy policies and terms may apply. I will aim to use project access only for the agreed work and will not sell your personal information.

Code and AI tools

If AI-enabled tools are considered for a project, the tools and the type of information shared should be assessed for that project. Please do not send passwords, secret keys, or other sensitive credentials in a contact form or ordinary email. Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for communication, project delivery, business records, or legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

You may contact me to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information I hold, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements. You can revoke project access you previously granted, although this may affect my ability to continue the work.

This policy may be updated as the website or services change. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.
